Professional profile

Connecting architecture with physical behavior

A background in aeronautical engineering, applied simulation, and customer-facing technical delivery.

My work connects two complementary disciplines: model-based systems engineering (MBSE), which captures system structure and relationships, and model-based design, which explores how physical systems behave. I use systems models and simulation tools to support engineering decisions across mechanical, thermal, and fluid domains.

At Boeing India, my primary work focuses on modernizing product lifecycle management (PLM) for product data management (PDM) systems and developing architectures for predictive maintenance. My broader engineering contributions include SysML modeling, digital twin and digital thread frameworks, and proofs of concept using Julia and Modelica.

Previously at Modelon Engineering, I combined simulation work with customer training and technical support, helping OEM teams use modeling tools from onboarding through advanced applications. Alongside this delivery work, I contribute to research on simulation model preservation and reuse through Modelica, FMI, and LOTAR communities.

Architecture & lifecycle

Systems and enterprise architecture using SysML, TOGAF, and the Unified Architecture Framework (UAF), with a focus on PLM modernization and digital continuity.

Applied physical modeling

Thermal and fluid simulation for battery management, HVAC, fuel systems, and environmental control, supported by Modelica and simulation integration tools.

Technical collaboration

Hands-on customer training, pre-sales and post-sales support, and collaboration with OEMs on long-term archiving and reuse of engineering models.
